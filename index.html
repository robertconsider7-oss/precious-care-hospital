Steps for a Routine Hospital Visit

  1. Review the department and clinic schedule.
  2. Contact the hospital or submit an appointment request.
  3. Wait for confirmation before relying on a clinic time.
  4. Arrive at the confirmed time and complete registration.
  5. Follow the instructions given by hospital staff.

What Patients Should Bring

  • Valid identification, where applicable.
  • Relevant previous medical records.
  • Referral documents, if applicable.
  • A list of medicines currently taken, if relevant.
  • Appointment confirmation details.
